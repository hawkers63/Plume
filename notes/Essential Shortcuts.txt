**bjr (bonjour)**

**Meaning:** Hello/Good morning

**stp / svp (s'il te plaît / s'il vous plaît)**

**Meaning:** Please (informal/formal)

**Example:** "Please call me."

**Meaning:** I can’t take it anymore / I’m overwhelmed (either from laughter or exhaustion)

**Formality:** Very informal / Popular among Gen Z

**mtn (maintenant)**

**Example:** "Where is he?" "I don’t know."

**oklm (au calme)**

### Slang in Texting

**Verlan (Backward Slang)**  
French features a distinctive form of backward slang (verlan):

- **chelou** = **louche** (weird)  
- **ouf** = **fou** (crazy)  
- **meuf** = **femme** (woman/girl)  
- **keuf** = **flic** (cop)  
- **relou** = **lourd** (annoying)  

---

**Street Slang in Texts**  

- **wesh:** Hey/Yo (derived from Arabic)  
- **stylé:** Cool/Stylish  
- **grave:** Seriously/Totally  
- **kiffer:** To love/like  
- **chanmé:** Cool (verlan of "méchant")  
- **boloss:** Loser/idiot  

---

**Gen Z Specific**  

- **sah:** I swear (from Arabic "wallah")  
- **askip:** Apparently (à ce qu'il paraît)  
- **frr:** For real (influenced by English, but written in French)  
- **bg (beau gosse):** Handsome guy  
- **s/o:** Shout out
